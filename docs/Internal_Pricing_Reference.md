# Internal Pricing Reference (Founder Only)

**Do not move or copy this file into `knowledge/`.** It exists specifically
*outside* the `knowledge/` tree so Bray (the AI sales agent) can never load
or quote it — see `knowledge/kaivix/pricing.md` for the customer-facing
pricing-conversation policy Bray actually follows, which intentionally
contains no real numbers.

This is the founder's own reference for quoting firm prices on discovery
calls once a visitor has been qualified.

---

## AI Employee — Lead Qualification Agent

- Setup fee: $5,000-7,500 one-time (non-refundable once development starts)
- Monthly retainer: $300-500/month (hosting, monitoring, maintenance, updates)

---

## AI Employee — Customer Support Agent

- Setup fee: $15,000 one-time
- Monthly retainer: $750/month

---

## Voice AI Agent

- Setup fee: $25,000 one-time
- Monthly retainer: $1,200/month

---

## Custom AI Software

- Quoted individually based on scope, no starting figure — this tier is
  scoped case-by-case and often exceeds the Voice AI Agent price

---

## Payments

- A 50% deposit is collected before work begins on a project.
- The rest of the payment schedule beyond the deposit is not yet documented here — confirm with the founder before quoting it on a call.
