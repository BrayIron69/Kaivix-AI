# Project Process

Kaivix Labs follows a structured process to ensure every AI solution is designed, developed, and deployed successfully.

---

## 1. Discovery

The first step is understanding the client's business.

During this stage we discuss:

- Business goals
- Current workflow
- Existing challenges
- Desired outcomes
- Budget
- Timeline

---

## 2. Solution Planning

After gathering requirements, we design the most suitable AI solution.

This includes:

- Selecting the right AI approach
- Planning integrations
- Defining project scope
- Preparing a project proposal

---

## 3. Development

Once the proposal is approved, development begins.

Depending on the project, this may include:

- AI agent development
- Workflow automation
- CRM integration
- API integration
- Dashboard creation
- Internal testing

---

## 4. Testing

Before deployment, the solution is thoroughly tested to ensure it performs reliably.

Testing includes:

- Functional testing
- Workflow validation
- Error handling
- Performance verification

---

## 5. Deployment

After successful testing, the solution is deployed to the client's environment.

Deployment may include:

- Installation
- Configuration
- Integration
- User onboarding

---

## 6. Support & Improvements

After deployment, Kaivix Labs provides ongoing support when included in the project agreement.

Additional improvements, new features, and future enhancements can also be developed as business needs evolve.

---

## Communication

Throughout the project, clients receive regular progress updates and have opportunities to provide feedback before major milestones are completed.
