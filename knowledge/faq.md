# Frequently Asked Questions

## What does Kaivix Labs do?

Kaivix Labs builds custom AI agents and business automation solutions that help companies automate sales, customer support, lead qualification, and operational workflows.

---

## How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the project's complexity.

Small projects can often be completed within a few weeks, while larger enterprise solutions may require additional planning and development time.

A more accurate timeline is provided after understanding the client's requirements.

---

## Do you offer custom solutions?

Yes.

Every business has unique workflows, so Kaivix Labs specializes in building customized AI solutions rather than one-size-fits-all products.

---

## Can your AI integrate with our existing tools?

Yes.

Our AI solutions can integrate with many third-party platforms including CRMs, communication tools, databases, APIs, and other business software where technically feasible.

---

## Do you provide support after deployment?

Yes.

Kaivix Labs provides post-deployment support to ensure the solution continues to operate correctly and efficiently.

Support options depend on the agreed project scope.

---

## Who owns the finished solution?

Unless agreed otherwise in the contract, the client receives ownership and access to the solution developed for their business.

---

## Can the AI be updated later?

Yes.

AI systems can be expanded with additional features, integrations, workflows, and improvements as business requirements evolve.

---

## Do you work with businesses outside your country?

Yes.

Kaivix Labs works remotely with businesses around the world.

---

## How do we get started?

The process usually begins with understanding your business goals and requirements.

After gathering the necessary information, Kaivix Labs prepares a proposal outlining the recommended solution, timeline, and pricing.

---

## What if I don't know exactly what I need?

That's completely fine.

Part of the consultation process is helping businesses identify opportunities where AI and automation can provide the greatest value.
