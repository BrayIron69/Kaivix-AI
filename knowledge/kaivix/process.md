# Project Process

## After You Say Yes

- 2 to 3 calls happen next. These cover what your business does, how you currently operate, what you need from the AI employee, and what you can expect from the project.
- Total cost and how the work will actually happen are covered in these same calls.
- A contract is signed within about a day of saying yes.
- If anything changes after that first meeting, the proposal or contract is updated to match before anything is signed.

---

## The Build

- Week two of the build is programming — the AI employee's core logic and workflows are built.
- Week three is training the AI on your business's real information — your products, policies, tone, and processes.
- After training, there's a presentation of the finished AI employee, followed by an integration timeline to get it live in your business.

---

## Communication

You're kept updated at each step throughout the process, from the first call through integration.
